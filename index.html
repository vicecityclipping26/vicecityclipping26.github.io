ViceCity Clips

ViceCity Clips is a personal tool used by the creator of the @vicecity.clipping fan page to prepare short gaming clips (Grand Theft Auto) and publish them to the creator's own social media accounts.

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to my own YouTube channel.

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our own Privacy Policy.

What the app does

  • Finds popular moments from public game streams on Twitch and Kick and edits them into short vertical videos, always with a visible credit to the original streamer.
  • Every clip is reviewed and approved by hand before it is published.
  • Uploads approved clips to the creator's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API) and TikTok account (TikTok Content Posting API).

Who can use it

The app is used only by its developer to manage their own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access other people's accounts.
