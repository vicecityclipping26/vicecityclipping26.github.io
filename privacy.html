Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

ViceCity Clips ("the app") is a personal tool used by its developer to publish short gaming clips to the developer's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Who uses the app

The app is used only by its developer to manage their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access or collect data from any other users.

2. Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos to the developer's own channel, and the name of that channel (to confirm the correct account is signed in).
  • TikTok (via TikTok Login Kit / Content Posting API): basic account information of the developer's own account (such as account ID and display name) and permission to upload videos to that account.
  • Sign-in tokens: the access tokens Google and TikTok issue after sign-in.

3. How data is used

The data is used only to upload clips the developer has reviewed and approved, and to confirm which account is signed in. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer, in files readable only by the developer's user account. No data is stored on any server, and no copies are kept elsewhere.

5. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube and TikTok, as needed to upload videos.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use and can be deleted at any time by deleting the token files. Access can also be revoked at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions (also see Google security settings).
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

7. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Google API data (Limited Use)

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
